# $honchoy: 5-minute demo script

**Setup before you start:** open the live URL in a phone-sized browser window (or a real phone mirrored).
Clear data first (Settings → Delete all my data). Keep a second tab open on `/?demo` as a backup.

---

### 0:00–0:30 · The problem (no screen)
> "Many women in Bangladesh manage household money every day, often with irregular income, microloans and no safety net, but most finance apps are in English, full of jargon and not built for them. **$honchoy**, from *সঞ্চয়*, 'savings', is a warm, private money planner in Bangla and English."

Point at the logo: *"A coin with a petal edge: savings that grow."*

### 0:30–1:15 · Onboarding (Welcome → Income)
- Tap **বাং** → the whole screen switches to Bangla. Tap **EN** back. *"Every word, even the numbers, is translated."*
- Try **Get started** without ticking → a gentle message. Tick the **18+** box.
  > "We only confirm 18+. We never ask for an exact age or birthday, and all data stays on the phone."
- Income: choose **Irregular** → *"If your income changes, we plan a bigger safety net."*
- Add **Salary 15,000** + **Freelancing 5,000** (tailoring orders) → total updates live → **Save & continue**.

### 1:15–2:15 · Money: expenses & debts
- Add **Rent 6,000 (Fixed)**, then toggle **Variable** → **Food 3,000**.
- Add a debt: **Microloan, 30,000, 45%, 3,000/month**.
  > "Two warnings: the rate is unusually high for an MFI, and we show how much of income goes to debt: under 30% is fine, above 40% is risky."
- Drag the **payoff slider** to ৳6,000 → *"Debt-free in 6 months instead of 13, saving about ৳3,000 in interest."*

### 2:15–3:00 · Plan (the engine)
> "Now $honchoy builds the monthly plan by itself."
- Walk the waterfall: income − expenses − debt = **free to plan**.
- Show the three buckets: *"Because her safety net is still empty, most goes to the emergency fund first. As it fills up, more flows to goals."*
- Log a saving of the suggested amount → toast 🎉.

### 3:00–3:50 · Goals
- Emergency fund bar: *"6 months of essentials, because income is irregular."*
- Add goal **Sewing machine, 15,000, 3,000 saved** → drag the **monthly contribution** slider: the months and the target date update live.
- Investment planning: answer 3 questions → *"Educational, rule-based guidance only. We never push a product, and we tell her to build a safety net and clear costly debt first."*

### 3:50–4:30 · Home dashboard + Learn
- **Home**: health score out of 100, income vs expenses, and 3 plain-language insights written from *her* numbers.
- Switch to **বাং** to show insights in Bangla.
- **Learn**: open *"What is interest?"* card → short, jargon-free, with a "Try this" tip.
- **Scam check**: tick *"guaranteed high returns"* → **High risk** warning. *"The #1 way savings disappear is fraud."*

### 4:30–5:00 · How it's built + close
> "It's plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with one shared data schema and one tested calculation engine. It is fully responsive and deployed on [Vercel/Netlify] from our GitHub repo. No account is needed and no data leaves the phone."
> "**$honchoy: your savings, your strength. আপনার সঞ্চয়, আপনার শক্তি।**"
